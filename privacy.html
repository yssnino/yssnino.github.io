Privacy policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information Nino Travel ("we") collects when you use this website, and how we use it. We keep collection to a minimum.

Searches

Search forms run in your browser. When you search, we build a link to a partner site and open it. We don't store your searches on our servers. To suggest airports as you type, your typed text is sent to Travelpayouts' public autocomplete service. Partner sites you visit have their own privacy policies and may use cookies, including to credit us for referrals.

Deal-alert sign-ups

If you join our deal alerts, we collect your email address to send you travel deals and tips. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email. We don't sell your email address.

Purchases

Payments for our guides and trip plans are processed by Stripe. We receive your name, email, the details you enter for your trip plan, and payment status, but never your full card number. See Stripe's privacy policy. We use trip details only to create and deliver your plan and to handle support.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Our Kids' Zone games run entirely in the browser and collect no personal information. Our services are intended for adults; we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can ask us to access or delete information we hold about you by contacting us via a direct message to @yssn_nino1 on TikTok.

Changes

We'll update this page if our practices change.

Heads up: Nino Travel is a search and inspiration site, not a travel agency. When you book through our partner links we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. How we make money
