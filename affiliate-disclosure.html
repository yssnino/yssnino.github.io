Affiliate disclosure

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Short version: We may earn a commission when you book through links on this site, at no extra cost to you.

Nino Travel is a free travel search and inspiration site run by YSSN Nino. We are not a travel agency, airline, hotel, or tour operator, and we don't sell travel. When you search or click a partner link, you're taken to the partner's website (for example Aviasales, Booking.com, Expedia, GetYourGuide, Viator, Discover Cars, or Airalo), and you book and pay there.

How we earn money

Many of our links are affiliate links. If you make a qualifying booking after clicking one, the partner may pay us a commission. The price you pay is the same as going to the partner directly. Commissions help keep our free tools, guides, and Kids' Zone running.

We also sell our own digital guides and trip-planning services through Stripe. Those are clearly priced and labeled.

Our promises

  • We don't publish fake reviews, ratings, or made-up prices.
  • A commission doesn't change what we recommend. If a partner isn't right for a trip, we'll say so.
  • Partner inclusion doesn't mean the partner endorses Nino Travel.
  • When we post about travel on social media using affiliate links or brand partnerships, we disclose it.

Who handles your booking

Your contract for flights, stays, cars, activities, or eSIMs is with the partner or the travel provider, not with Nino Travel. Prices, availability, payment, changes, cancellations, and refunds are handled by them.
