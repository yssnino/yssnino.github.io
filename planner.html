Hidden costs to check before you book

  • Hotels: taxes, resort or destination fees, parking, and Wi-Fi are sometimes shown only at checkout.
  • Flights: carry-on and checked bags, seat selection to sit together, and change fees on basic fares.
  • Cars: deposits, young-driver fees, tolls, fuel policy, and insurance.
  • Activities: per-person pricing for kids, transport to the venue, and cancellation terms.
  • Abroad: foreign transaction fees, phone data, tourist taxes, and tipping customs.
