How plans are made: we use AI tools to speed up research and drafting, and a person reviews every plan before delivery. AI-assisted content can contain mistakes, so always confirm prices, opening hours, and entry rules with the provider or official source before you book or travel.

Plans include: a day-by-day itinerary with links and an estimated budget. Personalized and Family plans add destination ideas, a shortlist of flights, stays, and activities with a total-cost estimate, and packing tips. Family plans add kid-friendly pacing and a family packing list.

What we need from you: at checkout you'll be asked for your destination, dates, travelers, and budget. If we need more detail, we'll email you at the address you used at checkout. The delivery clock starts once we have the information we need.

What's not included: we don't make, hold, or pay for bookings, and we can't guarantee prices or availability, which change constantly. We don't provide visa, immigration, medical, or legal advice. Some links in your plan may be affiliate links, which may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you.

Revisions are changes to the delivered plan for the same trip (for example, swapping activities or changing the pace). A new destination or new dates count as a new plan.

Our guides and printables are original PDF files for personal use. After checkout you're taken to a download page; see our delivery policy. Please don't share or resell the files.
